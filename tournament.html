Fixtures & results

Standings

3 points win · 2 points draw · 1 point loss. Ranked by points, then rounders difference, then rounders scored. Only completed group/league fixtures count in these tables.

Knockout progression

Knockout fixtures are listed by round. Advancing winners is a manual organiser action in this demo. Drawn knockouts require a tournament-specific tie-break rule.

Tournament data is local and can be cleared by Safari. Keep an external record of any real event. Never use this demo to publish official results.
